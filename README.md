# Supplementary Material — Governance-Compatible Gamification for Continuous Code-Quality Improvement

Supplementary package (S1–S6) for the paper:

> Linard, L., & Freitas, S. (2027). Governance-compatible gamification for continuous code-quality improvement: A design science case study in a large financial institution. *Proceedings of the 60th Hawaii International Conference on System Sciences (HICSS-60)*. Gamification minitrack.

The study was conducted at Banco do Brasil (low-platform development ecosystem). This package replaces the anonymized version used during double-blind review (HICSS-60 submission 12136); content is unchanged except for the removal of anonymization.

## Contents and mapping to the manuscript

| File | Contents | Supports |
|---|---|---|
| `S1_slr_protocol.md` | Full SLR protocol: PICOC, research questions, search-string blocks and query lattice, per-database exports, PRISMA 2020 selection flow (1,324 → 51), inclusion/exclusion criteria, quality-assessment checklist, data-extraction form | Section 3 (evidence stream 1) |
| `S1b_studies.md` | The 51 included primary studies (by quality tier) and the 30 studies excluded at full text, with aggregate exclusion reasons | Section 3 |
| `S2_imi_instrument.md` | Adapted IMI (22-item short version): items, referent, response scale, reverse scoring, fielded item order, per-item means, sampling plan and fielding summary | Sections 3 and 5 |
| `S3_octalysis_procedure.md` | Expert Octalysis assessment: judge panel composition, rating instrument and scale anchors, aggregation procedure, per-drive results | Section 5 |
| `S4_interview_script.md` | Structured interview script for the formative evaluation (English translation of the fielded script): consent preamble, guided-demonstration protocol, five constructs with anchor questions and probes | Section 7 |
| `S5_coding_scheme.md` | Thematic-analysis coding scheme: deductive codes (C1–C5), inductive codes, and the finding→revision traceability (R1–R5) | Section 7 |
| `S6_statistics.md` | Supplementary statistics: subscale correlation matrix with p-values and 95% CIs; interview perception scales with dispersion; Octalysis per-drive means | Sections 5 and 7 |

## Notes on completeness

- The respondent-level IMI export (53 × 22) was not retained, so per-subscale reliability coefficients cannot be reported. The instrument-level Cronbach's alpha (0.692) is reported in the manuscript.
- The judges' exercise survives only in consolidated form (rater-aligned disaggregation was not preserved), so **inter-rater agreement statistics are not computable and are not claimed**; `S3` documents the panel, instrument, scale anchors, aggregation, and per-drive dispersion, and states this limitation explicitly.
- Interview transcripts are not shared to protect participant confidentiality; the script (`S4`), coding scheme (`S5`), and per-construct rating distributions (`S6`) are provided instead.
- Launch Center is proprietary software of Banco do Brasil and is not publicly released.
